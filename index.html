BUILT FOR RUGGED CONDITIONS

Engineering that doesn't pretend Nigeria has 100% grid power.

Most commercial agritech solutions assume steady 220V power, stable Wi-Fi, and managers who check smartphone apps at 3:00 AM. AgriSense Sentinel is engineered around reality.

100% Solar & Battery

Mount the solar panel on the pen's roof. Integrated intelligent charge controller keeps the internal Li-ion pack charged with 3 full days of overcast backup.

Direct GSM SMS Alerts

Delivers urgent text notifications via MTN, Airtel, or Glo directly to any basic feature phone (Nokia "torchlight") without requiring data bundles or apps.

110dB On-Site Siren

If your phone is on silent or you're sleeping in the farmhouse compound, the on-site siren sounds immediately, giving night handlers instant notice to act.

Offline Black-Box SD Log

Every minute of environmental history is timestamped and stored locally on high-endurance SD media. Review seasonal curves even in zero-cell-coverage valleys.

IP65 Enclosure & Gas Filter

Pen air is filled with corrosive ammonia vapor and dust. Our conformal-coated board and IP65 enclosure shield internal micro-electronics from humidity and dirt.

Automated Fan/Mister Relay

Optional onboard relay switches on exhaust fans or misters automatically the split-second thresholds cross, protecting livestock even before staff arrive.
