Heat and ammonia don't wait for morning.

Pen conditions turn dangerous fastest between midnight and dawn — exactly when no one is watching. By the time a farmer wakes for the first check, the damage is often already done.

Monitoring systems built for commercial farms cost $500–$2,000 and assume Wi-Fi and steady grid power — two things most pens in Northern Nigeria don't have. Farmers are left to do the monitoring themselves, at 2am, by torchlight.

The Sentinel

One box. Solar power. Zero connectivity required.

The AgriSense Sentinel mounts inside the pen and runs on sun and a small battery. It never needs your Wi-Fi, your phone, or a data plan — it just watches, and speaks up the moment something is wrong.

  • Reads temperature, humidity, and ammonia continuously, day and night.
  • Sounds a siren on the spot the instant a threshold is crossed — no phone needed to hear it.
  • Sends an SMS straight to a basic phone over MTN, Airtel, or Glo — no smartphone or app required.
  • Keeps a full log on its SD card, so a bad night can be reviewed and understood afterward.
  • Sealed in a weatherproof IP65 shell built for a pen, not an office.
From mounting to morning

How a Sentinel earns its keep

Install

Mount the unit inside the pen. The solar panel and battery mean there's no wiring to run and no outlet to find.

Monitor

The Sentinel reads temperature, humidity, and ammonia around the clock, comparing every reading against safe thresholds.

Alert

The moment a reading turns dangerous, the on-site siren sounds and an SMS goes out — in time to act, not to grieve.

Built for the pen, not the lab

What's inside

Controller
ESP32 development board running MicroPython firmware
Sensing
SHT31 temperature & humidity sensor, plus ammonia gas sensing
Alerting
On-site siren + SMS via SIM800L GSM module (MTN, Airtel, Glo)
Power
Solar panel with charge controller, backed by a LiPo battery — no grid connection needed
Logging
Every reading and event recorded locally to an SD card
Enclosure
Weather-resistant IP65-rated housing, built for pen conditions
Connectivity
None required — works fully offline, with no Wi-Fi or smartphone dependency
