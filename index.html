TECHNICAL RIGOR & PROTOTYPE SPECS

Built for Gombe's infrastructure,
offline-first by design.

Production-grade ESP32, Sensirion SHT31, and SIM800L 2G GSM cellular core. Designed to protect the farmer's flock even if cellular towers fail.

AgriSense Sentinel Core Board Assembly

ESP32 dual-core processor, Sensirion SHT31, MQ-135 probe, and SIM800L 2G GSM modem.

Dual-Core ESP32 Microcontroller (< $5 Component)

Executes MicroPython FreeRTOS sensor sampling every 10–30 seconds. Phase 1 rolling-average anomaly detection runs directly on-chip with zero extra hardware cost.

Sensirion SHT31 & Calibrated Ammonia Probe (< $6 Component)

Medical-grade ±0.2°C temperature & humidity precision paired with sensitive parts-per-million gas detection suited for deep-litter broiler & layer pens.

SIMCom SIM800L Industrial 2G GSM (< $8 Component)

Reliable 2G SMS dispatch over MTN, Airtel, and Glo. Penetrates galvanized corrugated zinc roofing that completely blocks 2.4GHz Wi-Fi signals in rural pens.

Offline Local Siren Defense + Solar MPPT Battery

On-site 110dB siren triggers instantly when thresholds are crossed — no network needed. Farm workers & neighbors hear it even if the GSM module or cell tower fails.
