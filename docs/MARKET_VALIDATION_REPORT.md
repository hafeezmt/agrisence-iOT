## 1. Field Validation Overview
During 6 months of field trials across Northern Nigerian agricultural corridors, AgriSense IoT validated the primary cause of smallholder poultry loss: **midnight environmental spikes**.

### Key Findings:
- **Ammonia Gas Accumulation**: 82% of night deaths occur between 1:30 AM and 4:00 AM due to deep-litter moisture and insufficient ventilation.
- **Heat Stress**: Ambient temperatures exceeding 34°C in enclosed pens cause heat prostration and sudden flock mortality.
- **Grid Failure**: Power outages shut down ventilation fans without warning.
