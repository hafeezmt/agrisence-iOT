## 1. Ammonia ($NH_3$) Toxicity Thresholds in Poultry

Ammonia gas is produced when uric acid in bird excreta reacts with water and bacterial enzymes in deep-litter pens.

| Ammonia Level | Physiological Impact on Birds | Action Protocol |
|---------------|-------------------------------|-----------------|
| **0 – 10 ppm** | **Optimal**: Birds breathe easily; full mucosal ciliary clearance active. | Normal ventilation. |
| **11 – 20 ppm**| **Mild Stress**: Slight irritation to respiratory tract; reduced feed conversion ratio (FCR). | Inspect wet litter spots near drinkers. |
| **21 – 25 ppm**| **Warning Threshold**: Mucus membrane swelling; vaccine uptake declines; airsacculitis vulnerability. | **AgriSense Amber SMS Alert**: Open side curtains or start low exhaust fan. |
| **> 25 ppm**   | **CRITICAL HAZARD**: Loss of tracheal cilia; blind corneal burns (keratoconjunctivitis); asphyxiation. | **AgriSense 110dB Siren & Emergency SMS**: Full exhaust fan relay forced on; immediate handler intervention required. |

## 2. Temperature & Humidity Index (THI) Matrix

Birds do not have sweat glands; they dissipate heat primarily through panting (evaporative cooling). High ambient humidity prevents evaporative heat loss.

| Ambient Temp (°C) | Safe Max Humidity (%RH) | Heat Stress Risk Level |
|-------------------|-------------------------|------------------------|
| **20°C – 26°C**   | Up to 75%               | **Comfort Zone** (Normal growth) |
| **27°C – 31°C**   | Up to 65%               | **Moderate**: Panting begins, water intake increases 2x |
| **32°C – 35°C**   | Up to 55%               | **Severe**: Prostration, wing spreading, reduced egg production |
| **> 35°C**        | Any level               | **LETHAL**: Mortality from heat stroke within 2 hours |

## 3. The "2:00 AM Phenomenon"

Why nocturnal losses occur so frequently:
1. **Litter Moisture Accumulation**: During night hours, relative humidity peaks as ambient air cools.
2. **Reduced Natural Draft**: Thermal air convection outside the pen drops to zero.
3. **Power Outages**: Generator fuel runs dry or PHCN grid drops unnoticed by sleeping handlers.
4. **Smothering Behavior**: Birds cluster together in corners away from drafts, concentrating carbon dioxide and ammonia in micro-pockets.
