## Executive Overview
AgriSense IoT develops autonomous, solar-powered edge monitoring solutions designed specifically for poultry pens in Northern Nigeria. By combining medical-grade micro-climate sensors, edge anomaly detection, 110dB on-site strobe sirens, and direct 2G GSM SMS alerting, AgriSense prevents night-time flock mortality caused by heat stress and toxic ammonia gas accumulation.

## Key Value Proposition
- **Zero Internet Dependence**: Local dual-core ESP32 FreeRTOS edge evaluation eliminates reliance on unstable rural 3G/4G connectivity.
- **Immediate On-Site Defense**: Split-second 110dB siren triggers wake sleeping farm handlers before toxic gas smothering occurs.
- **Direct 2G GSM Reach**: Sends SMS alerts over GSM towers (MTN, Airtel, Glo) directly to basic feature phones.
- **Solar-Sustained**: MPPT charging paired with 72-hour battery reserve ensures uninterrupted operation through dust storms and power outages.
